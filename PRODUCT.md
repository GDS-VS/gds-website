# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing codebase: plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build tools, deployed via Netlify from GitHub (`GDS-VS/gds-website`, branch `main`). Fonts are self-hosted in `fonts/`.

## Users
(inferred from the project conversation, not freshly confirmed) Owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses in Villingen-Schwenningen and the Schwarzwald-Baar region (Donaueschingen, Bad Dürrheim, St. Georgen, Rottweil, Tuttlingen) who have no website or an outdated one and want to be found online and win customers. They are not technical; they prefer a person to a big agency.

## Product Purpose
GDS (Gürkan Digital Services) sells websites, SEO, redesign and maintenance. It exists so local businesses get a professional, findable web presence from one personal point of contact. Success: a qualified visitor books the free first consultation (Erstberatung).

## Positioning
A single local person (Mikail Gürkan) who works in person on site, instead of anonymous agencies or template builders; fixed contact person, reply usually within one working day, free first consultation.

## Operating Context
Contact via form (Formspree), phone and e-mail. Packages Basic 590 €, Advanced 1.290 €, Pro 2.490 € (one-off, indicative, plus VAT). Site is currently behind a client-side password preview gate and `noindex` until launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- German only. Du/Sie: always formal "Sie".
- Owner is a sole proprietor (Kleinunternehmer), copy speaks as "wir" (GDS).
- Legal pages exist: Impressum, Datenschutz (draft, texts still to be written).
- Prices and the three package contents must not change unless the owner asks.

## Brand Commitments
- Logo/wordmark "GDS." (Manrope 800, accent dot, subline "Webdesign & SEO") stays unchanged. The dot colour currently is `#7a7ae0`.
- Binding wishes from the owner in this project: calm premium agency look, nothing "AI slop", no typewriter/counting animations, no particle background, dashes as en dash (–), customers must not be shown that they are the first clients.
- Direction chosen for the redesign: a new colour world derived from the place (Schwarzwald-Baar), replacing the default navy/indigo.

## Evidence on Hand
No customer photos, no portrait of Mikail yet (placeholders exist), no real reference projects yet, no testimonials, no measured benchmarks. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles
1. Show a real person and honest local specifics before any decoration.
2. Claims must be provable; no invented numbers, customers or guarantees.
3. Plain German for non-technical business owners; value before price.
4. Calm, precise craft over effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keyboard operable with visible focus.
